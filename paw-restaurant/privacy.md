# Privacy Policy for Paw Restaurant

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Paw Restaurant ("the game") is published by MikGame ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and its service providers collect and how it is used.

### Information we collect

Game progress is stored on your device. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

The game and the services it uses may collect the following automatically: your device's advertising identifier, device model and operating system version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, app version, in-game events (for example levels reached, purchases and ads watched), and crash reports.

### How we use information

To run and improve the game, to show optional rewarded video ads, to process and restore in-app purchases, to understand how the game is used, and to find and fix crashes.

### Third-party services

Google AdMob, together with the mediation partners AppLovin and Unity Ads, shows optional rewarded video ads. These providers may use the advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice in the game settings. On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can decline and still use the game.

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collect usage statistics and crash reports.

RevenueCat processes purchase information (an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history) so that purchases can be validated and restored. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment card details.

On iOS the game can use Apple Game Center and iCloud to show leaderboards and back up your saved game.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

### Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

### Your choices and data deletion

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, change ad consent in the game settings where available, and decline notification permission. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. To ask about or delete data we hold about you, open an issue at https://github.com/minhnguyenduc19/minhnguyenduc19.github.io/issues

### Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
